01

Abstract

UOB SmartLine is a digital queue management system designed to reduce long waiting lines at university service offices. The system allows students to book tickets remotely, track their queue in real time, view estimated waiting time, and receive notifications when their turn is near. This improves the student experience and helps staff manage crowds more efficiently, resulting in smoother and more organized service operations.

02

Objectives

Reduce physical waiting lines.
Allow students to get digital tickets remotely.
Provide real-time queue position and ETA.
Notify students when their turn is near.
Help staff manage queues efficiently.
Improve the overall student service experience.
